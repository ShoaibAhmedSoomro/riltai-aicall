## Live Calls

Open **Monitor → Live Calls** to see every call running now in your organization: the agent, whether it is inbound or outbound, the other party's number, the step the call is in, and how long it has run. The list refreshes every few seconds.

Click **Watch** to follow one call. You see the same live transcript the person testing in the browser sees: what the caller says, what the agent says, tool calls, and each step change. If you open a call that started a while ago, the transcript so far is filled in first.

Any member of the organization can watch a call. A call that has ended, or whose agent crashed without finishing, is not listed.

### Listen in

Organization admins also get a **Listen in** button on a call. It plays the caller and the agent in your browser, a fraction of a second behind. Nothing is recorded by listening, and audio is only sent while the button is on.

Listen-in is **off** for a call when its agent's data policy withholds audio or redacts what is said (see [Data governance](/configurations/data-governance)), because live audio cannot be redacted. In that case the transcript is also governed by the same policy: an agent set to *basic only* shows its steps but not its words.

## Alerts

An alert rule watches your calls and tells you when something needs attention. Open **Monitor → Alerts**.

- **Alerts** is the feed of what your rules have caught. Acknowledge an alert to mark it seen.
- **Rules** say what to watch for.
- **Channels** say where to send alerts besides the feed: an email list or a webhook.

### What a rule can watch

| When | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| A call finishes | A call failed · Call duration · Call outcome is *(value)* · Call has tag *(value)* · Safety violation |
| Over a period | Calls in the window · Failure rate in the window · Average duration in the window |

Period rules look at the last *N* minutes and are checked every five minutes. A failure **rate** needs at least five finished calls in the period before it is judged, so two calls with one failure does not raise a 50% alert. To be told when calls stop coming in, use *Calls in the window* **is below** 1.

A rule can cover every agent or one. After it fires, it stays quiet for its **wait between alerts** (one hour by default), so a problem that persists does not send a message every five minutes.

### Channels

An **email** channel needs email to be set up on your deployment (the same setup as password reset emails). Without it, the channel cannot be created and the page says so. A **webhook** channel sends a JSON message to your URL, with optional authentication from your saved credentials. Use **Send test** on a channel to send a sample alert through the real path and see whether it arrived.

Failed deliveries are retried a few times with increasing delays, then parked; they appear in the failed-delivery count on the Reports page.

Alert messages say what happened, which agent, and link to the call. They do not include caller numbers or what was said.

<Note>
Creating rules and channels needs an organization admin. Everyone can read and acknowledge alerts.
</Note>
